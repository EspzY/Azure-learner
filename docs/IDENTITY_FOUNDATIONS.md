## Deterministic model contract

Network/DNS and customer AD DS control are explicit assumptions. Managed-domain credential readiness and synced/local changes remain distinct. No implicit federation is added to AD DS, and no reverse managed-domain sync is invented.

Sign-in: identity/trust, declared registration/verifiability, new method, suitable prior context, requirement and app action. Two knowledge secrets remain one category. SMS first-factor is passwordless but single-factor. Registered passkey/Hello with local verification can supply MFA; ordinary Authenticator phone sign-in is not FIDO2 phishing resistance. The Hello step-up example additionally assumes the current documented FIDO2/passkey prerequisites. An adequate existing context bypasses the new selected method; the result says that method was not checked. Identity proof, requirement acceptance, unknown/denied/allowed app action and actual app response are separate. Actual sign-in/token flags remain false and app response null.

External: the declared goals are a partner custom app, a shared channel from home without guest, or separate customer lifecycle. These constrain fit; they are not universal judgments about all B2B/customer architectures. Home/customer-IdP proof is shown separately from acceptance in the chosen resource context: verified home proof does not establish a missing B2B guest or collaboration path. B2B requires an already established representation, relevant home proof and applicable collaboration conditions. Permission to invite creates no object. Blocking new invitations does not by itself revoke an existing guest. Entra cross-tenant directions do not apply identically to a configured non-Entra IdP. Direct connect remains scoped to the declared supported Entra shared-channel mechanism. Customer uses its separate external configuration.
