## Bounded contracts

A versioned sequence has stable ID, official module reference, objective/source IDs, speaking NPC and finite beats. Beats have category, original text, camera intent, a registered visual cue and auto versus explicit-interaction pacing. No expressions, HTML, scripts or remote lesson body execute. Cue registry v1 supports an original provisioning flow, the reviewed responsibility matrix/model, and existing pure capacity/topology/meter/pricing examples. Teaching examples are visibly labelled and separated from saved learner experiments.

Playback owns only cursor/history, pause and reading preferences. Reading duration depends on words, punctuation and technical terms with a conservative minimum. Next/previous/replay are interruptible; manual mode and pause are always available. Questions/quest acceptance/experiments cannot advance on a timer. A skipped sequence opens activity choices but creates no practice. Past beats are a presentation history, not evidence. Changing script version requires review, not migration of old successful answers.

Timers are cancelled on beat/sequence changes, unmount, pause, hidden tab, journal/log/other modal and explicit investigation. A stale timer cannot advance a later conversation. Resume restarts a full readable interval, rather than stealing time spent elsewhere. Camera/demo updates are allowlisted transient projections; no story/model/probe/award event is dispatched by playback. Scene frame work remains imperative.
