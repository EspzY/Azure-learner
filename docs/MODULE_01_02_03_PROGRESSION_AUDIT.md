## Prerequisites and progression

Everyday browser → server/application → CPU/RAM/storage/network/OS → virtualization/provisioning → shared work/deployment/consumption → capacity versus fault tolerance → dependencies/recovery/predictability → permission/policy/consistency/resource efficiency → service-specific architecture and operational choices. Define redundancy, instance, routing, dependency, recovery, forecast, principal/role/scope, template, capacity-hour, vCPU, NIC, runtime, license and administrative role before their controls require them.

M1 retains every existing manuscript and official outcome. Its default responsibility visual focuses on the three already-authored practical incidents. A targeted link leads to the full service comparison; M1's original practice/assessment remains pinned. M2 owns new bounded experiments and a separate record. M3 reuses the original manuscript, full matrix and Portal, adding original operational activities and separately versioned evidence. The old Service Types presentation remains a clearly labelled archive and intentional resume choice.
